Fault Lines options

Stored locally on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere by the extension itself.

Examples: your Vercel deployment, a school server, GitHub Pages build, or http://localhost:3000 while developing.

Privacy

The extension only reads text you explicitly select or paste, only when you click. Answers are placed in the opened tab's URL hash (never sent to a server by the extension, and URL hashes are not transmitted to web servers). Permissions used: activeTab + scripting (read your selection on click), contextMenus (right-click send), storage (remember the app address). No tracking, no remote code.
